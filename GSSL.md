## 1. Executive Summary

Global Sight Services Limited (GSSL) is a Nigerian business-development, consultancy and capacity-building firm operating across insurance facilitation, artisan/driver verification technology, state government trade-data collation, and grassroots empowerment programmes. The current site is a WordPress build (Slider Revolution) with visible legacy branding artifacts from a previous template ("Sidmach") still present in titles, footers and copyright lines — a clear cleanup item for the redesign.

GSSL's value proposition centers on three pillars:

1. Verification/registration portals for artisans, drivers and hotel lodgers;
2. Insurance facilitation products (GPA, business insurance, passenger insurance); and
3. Government-partnered capacity-building and trade programmes (Ise Dotun, NATCO, state data collation).

The brand has over a decade of grassroots experience and claims impact on 300,000+ lives across multiple Nigerian states.

> **Note:** The current live site mixes GSSL's own copy with unrelated "Sidmach Technologies" template remnants (page titles, footer copyright, and some internal links resolve to a different company name). This should be fully purged in the redesign — flagged throughout this document as **[LEGACY ARTIFACT]**.

## 3. Our Story / About GSSL

Global Sight Services Limited (GSSL) is a Nigerian-based consulting and project implementation firm committed to delivering innovative, practical, and sustainable solutions that empower businesses, MSMEs, and government institutions across the country.

With over a decade of experience, GSSL specialises in financial services, project development, business support, and grassroots empowerment initiatives. The company is positioned as an identifier of operational gaps, a facilitator of access to finance, and a hands-on partner supporting MSMEs with digital tools and structured training.

GSSL describes its work as spanning "diverse sectors — from managing development projects and facilitating access to finance, to supporting MSMEs with digital tools and structured training," serving public institutions, private enterprises, and individuals seeking clarity, structure and results.

**What We Do**
Project development, financial advisory, capacity building, and tailored solutions that solve real challenges — from fieldwork to strategy, helping clients move from idea to impact.

**Who We Help**
Artisans, entrepreneurs, small businesses, government agencies, and community-based organisations — from early-stage operators to businesses ready to scale.

**Why Choose GSSL**
Positioned on local-market understanding, professionalism, and deep community roots, with an emphasis on trust and results-driven delivery.

## 7. Flagship Programmes & Real-World Initiatives

Beyond software products, GSSL runs large-scale, government- and corporate-partnered programmes on the ground. These are the strongest "proof of impact" content for the redesign and should anchor a dedicated Projects/Impact section.

**Ise Dotun**
A business development initiative for traders, artisans, cooperatives and small-scale industrialists in Oyo State, aimed at promoting economic growth and development in the state. Delivered in partnership with the Oyo State Ministry of Investment, Trade, Cooperatives and Industry (office located at the Oyo State Secretariat, Agodi, Ibadan). Its practical delivery vehicle is the Oyo State Business Insurance product (Fire & Special Perils).

**NATCO — Nigerian Artisans and Technicians Conference & Exhibition**
GSSL is the organising company behind NATCO, described on the site as "an important annual gathering in Nigeria's automotive sector." Per the client brief, NATCO has been hosted across Kano, Bauchi, Oyo and Lagos States. Recent event recaps referenced in the site's News & Insights include artisan and association award ceremonies, a Minister's representative receiving an honour on the Minister's behalf, an address by GSSL's MD/CEO to artisans, and an event described as drawing "thousands of artisans" to Adamasingba Stadium (Ibadan, Oyo State).

> *Note: the NATCO microsite (natco.com.ng) blocks automated content extraction, so its content should be manually reviewed and pulled directly by the design team for full event photography, sponsor logos, and past-conference detail.*

**Artisan Training Partnership with NNPC**
GSSL acted as a trusted implementation partner for an artisan training programme delivered in partnership with the Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation (NNPC), drawing on GSSL's grassroots development, project management, and community engagement experience. As part of this programme, NNPC — in collaboration with GSSL — presented gift items to artisans who completed their training, delivered in partnership with an entity referenced in the source material as "CARLOHA."

**Ise D'otun Regional Office**
Physical programme office located at the Oyo State Ministry of Investment, Trade, Cooperatives and Industry, Oyo State Secretariat, Agodi, Ibadan — the on-the-ground base for Ise Dotun and related state-partnered programmes.

## 11. Content Gaps & Redesign Opportunities

* **Legacy branding cleanup:** page titles, some footer links, and the copyright line still read "Sidmach" / "Sidmach Technologies Nigeria Limited" instead of GSSL — a full find-and-replace audit is needed.
* **Broken/placeholder links:** several nav and footer items (Nigerian Passengers' Insurance, Privacy Policy, "Others," some blog post links) point to empty anchors (`<>`) rather than live pages.
* **No readable partner/client logo alt-text:** homepage carousels need proper alt attributes and, ideally, a captioned partner grid instead of bare image sliders.
* **Single leadership bio published:** only the MD/CEO has a public profile — consider expanding to a fuller leadership/team section if additional bios are available.
* **Inconsistent product naming:** e.g., "Artisans' Verification Portal" vs. "Artisan Verification Software" vs. domain "naijaquickfix.com.ng" — unify naming conventions site-wide.
* **Corporate Profile PDF:** exists but should be re-hosted with a clean filename and checked for currency before relaunch.
* **NATCO microsite:** blocks automated crawling — content team should manually pull event photography, past-conference archives and sponsor listings directly from natco.com.ng.
* **Strong storytelling assets already exist** (300,000+ lives impacted, 10+ years, multi-state NATCO reach, NNPC partnership) but are scattered across banner text and blog posts rather than a unified "Impact" page — an opportunity for a compelling stats/impact section in the new design.
